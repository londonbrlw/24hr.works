Design Statement

*This portion would not be visible to the public eye, design statements are fun to make.*

24 hour art was designed to be simple, with a neutral color-scheme. This is so the background does not take away from the digital drawings. The first typeface is Work-Sans, known for being clean and legible. Playfair display was used for the headings, known for being elegant and artistic.

Logo Design:

The logo is in reference to Salvador Dali's piece on the persistence of time. Combining both artistic and time into one simplistic logo.

The first couple major iterations/sketches are shown to showcase the process. The original idea was the clock hands as art materials.
