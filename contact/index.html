About 24 Hr Works:

At midnight, all art is wiped from our database. Artists have 24 hours to draw, and post their art on here. At the end of the day, the top 5 pieces will remain online for another 24 hours.

The point of participating This is created for artists to detach from our art pieces, with a low stakes environment.
